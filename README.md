# isekAI

**Try on a job for a day.**

isekAI drops you into a realistic 15-minute workday in a role you've never
done — junior PM, senior PM, and more roles on the way. You get a real inbox,
a real chat with a boss, a coworker, and a client, and real decisions with
real trade-offs. No quiz, no "you're a visionary leader!" personality score —
just a day, and an honest reflection on what you actually did with it.

## Why

Career exploration today is mostly guessing: job titles, vague descriptions,
maybe a 20-minute informational interview if you're lucky. isekAI lets you
*feel* a role for 15 minutes before you spend a semester, a bootcamp, or a
job offer finding out it's not what you expected — or that it is.

## What a day looks like

![Clock in](screenshots/landing-clock-in.png)

You get a scenario, a seniority level, and a clock. Everything after that is
your call.

**Chat with a coworker** — data lives in conversation, not a dump. One
caveat at a time, and you still have to make the call.

![Chat with a coworker](screenshots/chat-with-coworker.png)

**Hear from a client** — over email, on their terms, telling you what the
room needs without writing the headline for you.

![Email with a client](screenshots/chat-with-client.jpg)

**Reflect, honestly** — when the day ends, each moment is named and played
back so you can decide for yourself how it felt. No fit score, no
archetype — your conclusion, not ours.

![Reflection map](screenshots/reflection-map-1.jpg)

**Share what you found** — an optional, evidence-grounded portrait of the
day, quoted from what you actually did, exportable as an image if it's worth
keeping.

![Portrait card](screenshots/user-portrait-card.jpg)

We also ask for quick, low-friction feedback at the end of every run — a
handful of sliders and short questions, nothing that takes more than a
minute.

![Feedback](screenshots/product-feedback.png)

## Try it

👉 **[isekai-web-pink.vercel.app](https://isekai-web-pink.vercel.app)** — no
signup beyond a name and email, ~15 minutes.

isekAI is closed-source while we're building it out — this repo is just the
front door. If you want early access, want to talk about what we're
building, or have feedback after trying it, reach out.

## Status

Early and actively growing — new roles, new scenarios, and a lot of
under-the-hood work on making the simulation feel honest rather than
scripted. More roles are coming.
